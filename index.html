About Seistech Imaging

We specialize in seismic imaging and structural geology, leading complex 3D projects across marine and land environments. Our core strength lies in bridging the gap between geophysical contractors and E&P teams, ensuring that cutting-edge technologies like Full Waveform Inversion (FWI) and velocity model building align strictly with geological model constraints, which directly mitigate exploration risks and deliver high-fidelity, exploration-ready products.

Seistech technical proficiency is further extended through specialized knowledge transfer, developing comprehensive curricula to enhance the cross-functional capabilities of geoscience teams.

Services

Technical consulting expertise

Specialist consulting across the full seismic imaging lifecycle — from project supervision and QC to advanced algorithm development and exploration risk reduction.

01

Seismic Imaging Supervision

Technical oversight of 3D seismic imaging projects in complex geological environments — including salt and mobile-shale tectonics, deep and shallow-water marine, and land settings. Assessment of geological consistency of seismic images and velocity models for prospects and drilling locations.

02

Velocity Model Building & FWI

Oversight of advanced velocity model building with Full Waveform Inversion (FWI) and tomography workflows. Ensures geological plausibility and alignment between the seismic velocity and the structural geological model of the area.

03

Interpretation-Driven Workflows

Design and implementation of interpretation-based de-migration/re-migration and seismic image-enhancement workflows that increase confidence in prospects and exploration opportunities.

04

Structural Geology & Shale Tectonics

Structural and seismic Interpretation of mobile-shale systems and salt tectonics in fold-thrust belts and sedimentary basins.

05

Exploration Team Integration

Bridging the gap between seismic processing contractors and E&P exploration teams — translating technical complexities into actionable insights and ensuring delivery of geologically meaningful results.

06

Algorithm Development & HPC

Leveraging Foundational Models to develop production-grade, high-performance seismic processing and imaging algorithms. Expertise in C/C++, Fortran, Python, OpenMP, and CUDA for GPU-accelerated processing on parallel computing platforms.
